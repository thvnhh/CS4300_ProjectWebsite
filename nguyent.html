A little about me...

My name is Thanh Nguyen, and I am a Computer Science major at the University of Houston - Downtown!
I am currently taking Web Programming where I am learning how to use HTML. Outside of school, I enjoy reading, writing, making art, and watching movies.
I find it hard to balance hobbies, school, and work-- but, I try my best! This summer instead of being on my phone all the time I tried to indulge in my hobbies instead.
Whilst doing so, I ended up watching a TON of movies and drew some new art (something I have not done in a while). One of the movies that surprisingly made me very sad was “About Time”.
The story follows a boy whose bloodline (only males) can time travel. It shows him falling in love, starting a family, and everything in between that comes with living life.
I found it quite endearing and real. No spoilers; however, I found the movie to be real and raw. Despite having the power of time travel, he had to deal with issues that you would think he would be invincible to or be
able to change the course of his life. I watch a lot of movies, and with that I also come across a lot of bad ones… I was very happy this movie connected with me!
